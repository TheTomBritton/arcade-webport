Light gun calibration

What this does

Tunes a Sinden (or any absolute-mouse light gun) for the arcade's games. Everything is done with the gun: every button on the calibration screen can be shot.

  1. Border — the white border the gun tracks, and whether the game draws its crosshair.
  2. Steadiness — measures how much the aim shakes, and tunes the smoothing that steadies it.
  3. Calibrate — shoot 9 targets; the games correct the aim to match.
  4. Check — shoot a few random targets with the correction on, and save.

Stand where you play. Your gun's driver must be running and treating the gun as a mouse. Settings are kept in this browser and apply to every game in the arcade, in fullscreen.

Esc leaves the calibration screen at any point; nothing is saved until the last step.
